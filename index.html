Shiftform

Climb an endless tower. Every few screens the world changes its rules and its look. Every metre is proven climbable. How high can you get?

Controls

←→ or ADMove ZSpace↑WJump. Hold for higher. Pressing just before landing still counts. XShiftDash, on levels that allow it FLift a crate, throw it while moving, set it down while still. In mid-air, grab a hook; jump or F lets go. RHEscBack to your last checkpoint, show the solver's route for this stretch, back to this menu

Objects

CratesPush by walking into them. Wood is light and floats, steel is heavy to carry, stone only moves when you dash into it and crushes saws. PlatesOpen their shutter or bridge only while something heavy sits on them. HooksSwing across gaps you cannot jump.

The climb

BiomesEvery few screens a spring launches you into a new biome with new rules. FlagsCheckpoints. Falls are real, but never below the biome you are in. LivesFive. Each death costs one, each new biome gives one back. Run out and the climb starts over. BarThe bar under the play area lists the biome's controls; new ones glow.
