Shiftform

Climb an endless tower. Every few screens the world changes its rules and its look. Every metre is proven climbable. How high can you get?

Controls

←→ or ADMove ZSpace↑WJump. Hold for higher. Pressing just before landing still counts. With your legs against a wall in mid-air, jump kicks you off it; the next kick must be off the other side. ↑↓ or WSOn a ladder, rope or vine, hold up to grab and climb, down to climb down. Jump leaps off; at the top, step sideways onto the platform. XShiftDash, on levels that allow it FLift a crate, throw it while moving, set it down while still. In mid-air, grab a hook; jump or F lets go. RHMEscBack to your last checkpoint, show the solver's route for this stretch, sound on or off, back to this menu

Objects

CratesPush by walking into them. Wood is light and floats, steel is heavy to carry, stone only moves when you dash into it and crushes saws. PlatesOpen their shutter or bridge only while something heavy sits on them. HooksSwing across gaps you cannot jump. LaddersLadders, ropes and vines carry you up walls too high to jump. TrampolinesStep on one to be launched far above; steer onto the ledge beside it. MonstersTouching one kills you. Land on top to squash it. Spitters' shots stop once the spitter is squashed.

The climb

BiomesEvery few screens a spring launches you into a new biome with new rules. FlagsCheckpoints. Falls are real, but never below the biome you are in. LivesFive. Each death costs one, each new biome gives one back. Run out and the climb starts over.

Items

WhereOn risky ledges off the main route. Skipping them is always safe. JetpackOut of jumps? Jump again in mid-air and hold to fly until the fuel runs out. BootsFive extra mid-air jumps. ShieldThe next spike, saw or laser bounces you to safety. HeartRefills your lives. CometRare. Fly up through platforms and bounce on every landing. Tap jump just as you land to bounce higher; miss and it burns out. Can carry you through two biomes. BarThe bar under the play area lists the biome's controls; new ones glow.
